Services

Care in Gynaecology and Obstetrics

Consultations and ongoing management for the concerns seen in her practice at Eesha Health Care.

High-Risk Obstetrics

Pregnancy care when there is a higher chance of complications, from the first visit through follow-up. Visits are planned around the mother’s health and the baby’s growth, and brought closer when either needs more review.

Infertility

Consultation and continued care for women and couples trying to conceive. The first visits cover both partners’ history, the cycle, and tests already done, then the treatment that fits. The same doctor follows the couple through each cycle.

IUI — Intrauterine Insemination

Prepared sperm is placed into the uterus around ovulation, often after medicines that help an egg mature, when the tubes are open and the sperm count is suitable — including unexplained Infertility or a mild Male Factor.

Laparoscopy and Hysteroscopy for Infertility and Gynaecology Conditions

Laparoscopy

Endometriosis, Adhesions, Ovarian Cysts, Fibroids and Tubal Problems.

Hysteroscopy

Polyps, Fibroids inside the cavity, a Septum, Adhesions and Abnormal Bleeding.

General Gynaecology

Routine consultations and review of conditions that commonly bring patients to clinic.

Menstrual Disorders

Abnormal Uterine Bleeding, heavy periods, irregular periods, painful periods and Amenorrhea.

Uterus and Ovaries

PCOD, Uterine Fibroids, Adenomyosis, Endometriosis and Ovarian Cysts.

Pelvic Health

Pelvic Pain, Pelvic Inflammatory Disease, Pelvic Organ Prolapse and Urinary Incontinence.

Infections

Vaginal Infections, Yeast Infection and Bacterial Vaginosis.

Cervix and Menopause

Cervical Abnormalities, HPV-related conditions, Menopause, Vaginal Dryness and Female Sexual Dysfunction.

Breast Concerns

Breast Lumps assessed as part of Gynaecology care.

About the doctor

Gynaecology and Obstetric Care in Visakhapatnam

Dr. P.V. Suneetha is an Obstetrician and Gynaecologist. She completed her M.D. in Obstetrics and Gynaecology at AIIMS, New Delhi, and her day-to-day work covers general Gynaecology together with Infertility and High-Risk Obstetrics. She is doing postdoctoral training in Infertility with Dr. Sadhana Kantilal Desai at Dr. Desai Clinic — IVF Centre, Gamdevi, Mumbai.

Patients consult her at Eesha Health Care, Jagadamba Junction, and she continues to see new patients for Gynaecology care.
